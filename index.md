I am an observational astrophysicist and a postdoctoral researcher at Michigan State University.  
My research investigates how the dynamics of globular clusters shape their compact binaries. I work with X-ray, optical, and radio observations and Monte Carlo globular cluster simulations:

**X-ray Populations:** I built GCXS, a uniform Chandra catalogue of 2,144 X-ray sources in 88 Milky Way globular clusters (submitted to MNRAS). I have also carried out multi-epoch X-ray studies of individual clusters.

**Multi-wavelength Observations:** I lead studies that join Chandra data with optical spectroscopy and radio observations, such as a two-decade study of an ultraluminous X-ray source in a globular cluster of NGC 1399. I am also PI of a VLA program on an ultra-compact dwarf.

**Cluster Dynamics:** I use pulsars and cataclysmic variables to trace how clusters evolve, and compare the observed populations with Monte Carlo globular cluster simulations.

**Data Analysis:** I use machine learning to classify X-ray sources in crowded fields, and Bayesian methods to find their HST and Gaia counterparts.
